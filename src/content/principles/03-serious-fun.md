A computer that makes you smile is a computer you want to use. Omarchy is built on the idea that "a beautiful system is a motivating system, and productivity has always been downstream from motivation." Fun isn't a garnish. It's part of why the work gets done.

Fun has one rule, though: it never costs a keystroke. Delight belongs in the margins, in the easter egg you stumble on, the screensaver while you're away, the joke in a name. The path to getting work done stays clear and fast.

## In practice

### Hide treasure, don't block the road

The best fun is discovered, not imposed. Reward curiosity with secrets, and leave the main path alone for anyone in a hurry.

**In Omarchy:** Tap `CapsLock`, `M`, and a letter to drop an emoji anywhere you can type. `CapsLock M X` gives you 🎉, and the manual's mnemonic for it is "xellebrate." ([Hotkeys](https://omarchy.org/manual/hotkeys/#quick-emojis))

### Give the idle moments personality

The moments when nobody is working are the best place for fun. Screensavers, About screens, and empty states can play as much as they like without costing anyone time.

**In Omarchy:** The screensaver runs ASCII art through random text effects, and you can make it say "ASCII art, your name, a rude word." On the About screen, "a glint of green leans across the art every few seconds and then leaves it still again." ([Branding](https://omarchy.org/manual/branding/))

### Let the fun step aside

When delight and function collide, function wins, without making a fuss about it. If an effect could break the layout, it should quietly not run.

**In Omarchy:** The About glint switches itself off for art made of emoji or double-width characters, because sliding across them would push the rest of the line out of place. The manual calls it "the animation keeping out of the way rather than failing." ([Branding](https://omarchy.org/manual/branding/#about-screen))

### Playful names, plain labels

Give your projects personality, but label actions by what they do. People search a menu for "calculator," not for a pun.

**In Omarchy:** Omarchy's own apps are called Omawrite, Omacut, Hype, and Monologue, but the hotkey list says "Writing (Omawrite)" and "Calculator," and the menu just says "Screenshot." ([Hotkeys](https://omarchy.org/manual/hotkeys/#launching-apps))

### Make fun optional

Delight you can't turn off becomes an annoyance by the hundredth time. Give every playful behavior an off switch.

**In Omarchy:** If you'd rather go straight from working to locked, `omarchy toggle screensaver` turns the idle screensaver off. ([Toggles](https://omarchy.org/manual/toggles-idle-screensaver/#the-screensaver))

### Serious craft for silly things

A joke done well is still done well. Build easter eggs with the same care as features: fast, aligned, themed, and handled properly when something goes wrong.

**In Omarchy:** `omarchy ascii` draws screensaver text in the FIGlet font the Omarchy wordmark is drawn in. That font has no digits or punctuation, so anything it can't draw is "dropped and named on stderr rather than quietly swallowed." Even the silly tools get proper error handling. ([Branding](https://omarchy.org/manual/branding/#words-instead-of-a-logo))

## Room to play

Fun is the most personal part of a computer, so it's the part people should own most completely. Ship a ridiculous theme. Put a rude word on your screensaver. Hide a game in your plugin. Write a pun song. Keep it out of the way of the work, and expect that someone, someday, will want to turn it off.

## Paper cuts

- An animation you have to sit through before you can keep working.
- A joke in an error message while someone is losing their work.
- A cute icon with no label, no tooltip, and no hotkey.
- Confetti on the critical path.
- Whimsy that falls apart in another theme or another font.
